ScanCam

PDF Scanner & OCR for Android. Private by default.

What it does

ScanCam turns your phone into a fast, private document scanner. Open the app, tap scan, and you have a shareable PDF in seconds. Nothing is uploaded. Nothing requires an account.

Features

  • Multi-page PDF scanning with automatic edge detection
  • On-device OCR for Latin, Devanagari, Chinese, Japanese, and Korean scripts
  • Searchable PDFs — Ctrl+F works in any PDF viewer
  • On-device translation to 50+ languages
  • Folders, favorites, and search over scan contents
  • Dark mode and dynamic color (Android 12+)

Privacy

Your scans stay on your device. Images, PDFs, and extracted text never leave your phone. No account, no sign-up, no email collection.
